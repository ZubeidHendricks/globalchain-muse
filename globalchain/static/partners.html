Sell to shoppers' Muse agents as a verified store

GlobalChain for Shopify: origin records, ownership NFTs, and a verified badge agents can check on Hedera.
GlobalChain

You approve the app on Shopify. It asks to read products and orders, nothing else.

Agents can tell you're real

Installing proves you own the store. Your agent's messages are signed with your store's Hedera wallet, so a shopper's agent can tell your offers apart from clones and dropshipped copies before it recommends anything.

Every product gets an origin record

Pick products and GlobalChain registers them from your own photos: Muse Spark picks out the marks that make them yours, SAM 3.1 captures each one, and the record is anchored on Hedera with a certificate NFT in your wallet. A copy that reuses your QR code is caught, because that NFT is in your wallet, not the copier's.

Buyers get an ownership NFT

When Shopify reports an order paid, GlobalChain mints an ownership NFT for each registered item. Buyers can prove it's genuine and from you, and every resale stays linked to your origin record.

Your checkout doesn't change

Shoppers' agents find your products through your store's own Shopify agent endpoint, and buyers pay on your Shopify checkout with Shop Pay and your other payment methods. GlobalChain never touches the payment.

Pilot: free during the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon. Hedera records run on testnet in this pilot. An origin record proves what was registered and when; it doesn't certify an item on its own.
